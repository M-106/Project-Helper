### Game Settings

Gaming on PC does require to set your own settings (because of the different hardware configurations - the freedom of a PC-Hardware) to get the optimum out of your hardware. Nice about that is that you can change your game so that it suits to you. Some may want better graphics fidelity and some others a smoother experience and here you can change in many games very much. For example I do not like super sharpen (with a artificial sharpen look) so I turn of every post-sharpening and use high edge smoothing also called Anti-Aliasing (AA), I prefer a smooth maybe a bit blur look above "artificial" sharpening look.

- in depth explanation of available game settings and what they mean
